Features

Everything an invoice needs. Nothing it doesn't.

The boring parts are automatic, so you can spend your time on customers instead of paperwork.

Numbering that stays in order

Every invoice takes the next number in sequence, per year. No duplicates, no gaps to explain later.

Totals you can trust

Subtotal, tax, and total are calculated the same way on every document, so nothing is miscounted.

Draft, sent, paid

Follow each bill through its status, so you always know what is waiting on you and what is waiting on the customer.

Customers saved once

Keep client names, addresses, and tax numbers in your workspace and reuse them on every invoice.

Your company on every page

Company name, address, logo, and bank details appear on each document, ready to send.

Ready to send or print

Clean, consistent layouts that read well on screen, in an email attachment, or on paper.

How it works

Three steps from new bill to paid.

Set up the details once, then billing a customer takes a couple of minutes.

  1. 01

    Add your company once

    Fill in your business name, address, logo, and bank details in your profile. Every invoice reuses them.

  2. 02

    Build the invoice

    Choose the customer, add line items, set tax and a due date. Subtotal and total update as you type.

  3. 03

    Send and track it

    Save the invoice, send it to your customer, then follow its status until the payment lands.
